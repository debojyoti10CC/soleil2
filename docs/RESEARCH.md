# Soleil — Research and Colosseum Track Strategy

Research checked October 1, 2026. Read with the [Soleil PRD](<C:/Users/Debojyoti De Majumde/Documents/Codex/2026-10-01/set-up-colosseum-copilot-for-this/outputs/Soleil-PRD.md>). This is an evidence and decision record, not an application, forecast of winnings or implementation schedule.

## 1. What the research supports

The original reserve-before-yield idea is technically plausible, but its primitive and headline overlap established products. The more useful hypothesis is an approved-invoice business workflow that exposes funding, locks the worker's fixed claim, survives an unavailable employer executor, and reconciles actual delivery.

The strongest evidence is technical feasibility. Customer willingness to buy an irreversible lockup remains unproven. Formation, banking and off-ramp services can extend the journey through providers; they are not consequences of deploying a contract.

Research combined the official Colosseum Copilot's winner-first project records, current organizer rules and FAQs, the resources hub, Grid product/organization checks, current competitor sites and first-party technical/legal documentation. Copilot's resources snapshot was marked stale, so consequential integrations were checked against their own current documentation. No paid research, external applications, provider contracts or customer interviews were performed.

### Recommended configuration

**Core: Solana-native funded invoice claims. Alternate: Tempo-native funded invoice claims.** Both use the same invoice lifecycle and independent liabilities. No bridge, token launch or cosmetic anchoring is needed.

This gives a coherent case for both ecosystems if both implementations work, and aligns the startup with the current accelerator's explicit Solana requirement. It does not establish that both awards can be received. If buyers prefer Tempo, let it be their operational default; do not claim contest alignment proves commercial fit.

Open-source the verification/settlement interface, invariant specification, receipt format and independently runnable example under an appropriate license. A commercial app can contribute useful open infrastructure, but Public Good eligibility and external use must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

The current accelerator advertises $250,000 per accepted startup through SAFE plus token warrant or STAMP. This is investment, not prize cash. A real Solana integration is currently required; admission is not guaranteed. The first two weeks and Demo Day require San Francisco attendance; founders pay flights and lodging. [Accelerator](https://colosseum.com/accelerator).

## 5. Current competitive reality

### Stablecorp: possible partner and direct overlap

Use the exact entity at **mystablecorp.xyz**, linked to Colosseum's Stablecorp project. It is not the unrelated Canadian stablecoin business with the same name.

The live [pricing page](https://mystablecorp.xyz/pricing) shows LLC formation at $999 and Delaware C-Corp at $1,199; ongoing compliance starts at $299/year with variable scope. The payroll tab lists USD and USDC contractor payroll at 1%, with direct INR settlement. These are provider prices/claims checked in the public interface, not a binding quote for a Soleil partnership.

Its [terms](https://mystablecorp.xyz/terms), updated August 13, 2026, include contractor payroll, treasury and W-9/1099-NEC support. Its [security disclosure](https://mystablecorp.xyz/security) limits provider-access promises and makes clear that approvals and every US/home-country obligation are not guaranteed.

The public [MCP page](https://mystablecorp.xyz/mcp) describes formation intake and knowledge responses. It is not evidence of a production payroll/formation API with webhooks. Before promising an embedded integration, obtain actual API documentation, terms, roles, failure states and permission to use it commercially.

The Copilot resource card advertised a 30% incorporation perk and related onboarding support. The resource snapshot was stale: treat that as a lead to verify, not a quoted discount, confirmed entitlement or cash prize. [Colosseum resource hub](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair/resources).

### Rise and Request: baseline capabilities are mature

[Rise's March 2026 announcement](https://www.riseworks.io/blog/rise-launches-built-in-stablecoin-yield-for-global-teams-and-companies-via-aave-vaults) describes yield for both employer balances and workers through Aave. [Rise Earn](https://www.riseworks.io/products/rise-earn) and [stablecoin payroll](https://www.riseworks.io/products/stablecoin-payroll) already occupy the broad payroll/yield proposition.

[Request contractor payments](https://www.requestfinance.com/use-cases/global-contractor-payments) and [stablecoin payments](https://www.requestfinance.com/products/stablecoin-payments) establish that invoicing, approvals and batching are not new. Soleil must offer a measurably better funded-obligation/settlement workflow; no comprehensive competitor feature audit established that every alternative lacks it.

### Sablier: avoid a false novelty claim

[Sablier V2](https://blog.sablier.com/introducing-sablier-v2) includes noncancelable prefunded streams and configurable unlock schedules. Soleil is not first to prevent an employer from canceling a funded claim. Its hypothesis is an opinionated business experience, private invoice records, recipient onboarding, reliable delivery reconciliation and appropriate provider cashout.

Sablier Labs' [maintenance-mode announcement](https://blog.sablier.com/sablier-labs-is-entering-maintenance-mode) also cautions against assuming open payment primitives alone support a venture business. The deployed contracts remain a meaningful alternative.

| Does supported-country eligibility mean INR cashout? | No. Country onboarding and payment routes are different dimensions. [Country list](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/customers/compliance/supported-countries-list), [payment routes](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/get-started/introduction/what-we-support/payment-routes). |
| Can Soleil count a fiat deposit notification as claim backing? | No. Only the exact asset actually present in the selected native vault backs its liability. |
| Does an off-chain paid badge prove delivery? | No. The specific canonical settlement must establish intended-recipient delivery. |
| Does a company formation provider cover every ongoing tax obligation? | No. Obtain exact filing/service scope; neither registration nor dashboard tasks prove filings. |

[Atlas signup](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas/signup.md) supports real formation/equity workflows. [Atlas pricing](https://stripe.com/atlas) advertises $500 including the initial registered-agent year, with $100/year thereafter; compare total service scope rather than just initial price. These costs and services are not a recommendation that every customer needs a US company.

Bridge's [business verification requirements](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/customers/compliance/businesses/overview) include legal/ownership and operating information; provider requests can vary by risk and route. Keep identification evidence in hosted verification where possible, and distinguish approval from a returned browser redirect.

Legal accuracy matters: current [FinCEN BOI guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) exempts US-created domestic entities under the revised rule; banking ownership checks remain separate. [IRS Form 5472 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) illustrate material reporting duties for applicable foreign-owned entities. [FinCEN virtual-currency guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/guidance/application-fincens-regulations-certain-business-models) makes the actual business model and control relevant. Do not automate legal conclusions from the word “noncustodial.”

## 7. Technical findings that must survive implementation

1. **Coverage is chain-local.** One chain's balance and one asset's dollar price do not cover another chain/token's obligation.
2. **Balance is not transfer eligibility.** Issuer freezes and recipient restrictions can block otherwise funded payments.
3. **Tempo success can be misleading.** Receive-policy redirection requires atomic intended-recipient verification. [Tempo receive policies](https://tempo.xyz/developers/docs/guide/payments/configure-receive-policies).
4. **Solana PDA control is not sufficient by itself.** Validate account derivations, owners, mint, token program, recipient and deployment authority. [PDAs](https://solana.com/docs/core/pda), [program authorities](https://solana.com/docs/core/programs).
5. **Unknown asset extensions break generic assumptions.** Start with the explicitly supported ordinary asset behavior and exact transfer checks. [Solana token transfers](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/transfer-tokens).
6. **Scheduling is not autonomous execution.** A funded submitter is still required. Sponsor fees and Solana rent are separate operating costs.
7. **Every outflow matters.** A reserve-checking strategy method cannot protect funds if a generic call, upgrade or standing allowance bypasses it.
8. **Yield liquidity must not be assumed.** Ondo's exact calling vault needs eligibility and a verified redemption path; its position value is not invoice backing. [Manager integration](https://docs.ondo.finance/developer-guides/usdy-instant-manager-integration).

## 8. Evidence needed to make a stronger winning case

The judging guidance considers working functionality, novelty, impact/UX, composability and business potential. Translate that into observable product evidence. [Judging FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon#h-faq-12).

| Claim | Strong evidence | Weak substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer needs Soleil | Buyer funds and uses accepted-invoice commitments repeatedly; observed admin savings | Friendly comments or invented market-size numbers |
| Worker can trust the receipt | Independent verifier reads native state and deployment authority | Screenshot of a database flag |
| Operator absence does not prevent payout | Disable normal executor and settle through another caller | Normal employer batch payment only |
| Both rails are substantive | Separately funded native claims and actual recipient delivery on each | A memo/anchor or wallet connection on the second chain |
| Formation/funding integration works | Actual provider-approved state, consented handoff and reconciled route | Successful mock form or unverified provider logo |
| Public infrastructure is reusable | Independent example/application works without Soleil's service | Repository publication alone |
| Treasury risk is isolated | Rejected reserve-breaking operation, invariant tests and clearly simulated loss | An APY display |
| Business can support itself | Customer price test and provider/sponsor/support cost model | Expected prize revenue or hypothetical yield spread |

## 10. Remaining uncertainties

Customer lockup incentive, production provider approval, exact chosen assets, home-country legal treatment, formation/payments partnership terms, multiple ecosystem-prize stacking and conditional prize eligibility remain unresolved. None is answered by more sponsor integrations.

The next decisive proof is the complete funded invoice and independently settled worker receipt, followed by observed buyer behavior. The PRD specifies the technical and service boundaries needed to make that proof credible.
